The Master Brewer's Guide ☕

Learn the art, then taste the perfection at CoffeePaglu.

⚗️

The French Press

For those who love a bold, heavy-bodied cup. It's simple, raw, and powerful.

  • 4 mins steeping time
  • Coarse ground coffee
  • Boiling water (95°C)
🌪️

The Pour Over

Clean, crisp, and aesthetic. This method brings out the hidden floral notes.

  • 3 mins brew time
  • Medium-fine grounds
  • Circular pouring motion
❄️

Paglu Cold Brew

Our signature obsession. Zero bitterness, maximum caffeine kick.

  • 18-24 hours steep
  • Room temp water
  • Double filtered

Pro Tip: Can't wait 18 hours? Grab one fresh at our shop!
